# Demo video script (under 3 minutes)

The hackathon rule is strict: a video longer than 3:00 loses prize eligibility. Aim for 2:45. Record the live deployment in a desktop browser at 1280 pixels wide or more, and speak the lines below or put them in your own words.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Home page | "Suppliers wait 30 to 90 days to be paid. Buyers sit on idle cash. Sooner lets the supplier pick the day they are paid, and the buyer earn the discount." |
| 0:15 | Drag the slider on the quote | "The price is the calendar. A thousand dollars due in 45 days is worth 985 today at 12% a year, and the amount rises every day until the due date." |
| 0:30 | Press **Start the demo**, wait for the four ticks | "The demo creates a buyer wallet and a supplier wallet on Solana devnet and funds them with test dollars." |
| 0:45 | Buyer desk: open a pool at 12%, add 5,000 | "The buyer opens a pool. The cash sits in a vault that only the program can pay from." |
| 1:05 | Approve an invoice: demo supplier, 1,000, 45 days, INV-1042 | "The buyer approves an invoice. Supplier, amount and due date go on-chain, and the rate is locked." |
| 1:25 | Switch to Supplier at the top; supplier desk | "Now I am the supplier. This number is what I can take this second, and it grows every second." |
| 1:40 | Open the invoice page; point at the chart; press **Take ... now** | "One click. The program prices the time, pays me from the vault and takes its fee, in one transaction." |
| 1:55 | Open the explorer link from the toast | "Settled in about a second, for a fraction of a cent." |
| 2:05 | Switch to Buyer; buyer desk figures | "The buyer kept the discount. That is a return on cash that was sitting idle." |
| 2:15 | Payment record page | "And every payment builds a public record of how this buyer pays, which nobody can edit." |
| 2:25 | README, "Why it is different" table | "Most on-chain invoice projects are lending: they need outside lenders, proof the invoice is real, and someone to absorb a default. Here the buyer who owes the money approves and funds it, so none of that is needed." |
| 2:40 | Team slide or you on camera | "I am [name], studying at [university]. [One sentence on why you chose this.] Sooner is open source and live on devnet. Thank you." |
